# Gambling industry research

Use public state reports and retained legal developments to judge **covered-market demand and competitive position for FLUT, DKNG and CZR**. The work is plain Python, pandas, SQLite and Jupyter, with offline analysis by default.

1. **Capture when needed:** [20 — collect official reports](notebooks/20_run_all_collectors.ipynb). Preview the exact sources, new dated destination and external backup before enabling a live run. Add a legal source/event using the small [industry guide](docs/industry_workflow.md).
2. **Read the monthly fundamentals review:** [94 — gambling industry update](notebooks/94_gaming_industry_update.ipynb). One notebook shows 12 monthly observations, fixed rolling-three-month comparisons, supporting and contrary evidence, changes from the prior monthly read, and source gaps. Demand, brand share, gross hold, casino and legal evidence remain separate.
3. **Investigate a question:** [90 — source explorer](notebooks/90_consolidated_ggr.ipynb). Inspect a particular state, native metric, period and retained source.

The [industry guide](docs/industry_workflow.md) explains the explicit snapshot selection, calculations and optional dated note export. See the [September 12 monthly review](docs/monthly_fundamentals_20260912.md) and [dated issuer/ownership context](docs/company_context_20260912.md). The [earlier July-only update](docs/industry_update_20260912.md) remains a historical reference.

The selected September 12 capture contains **19,806 observations across 34 state/product series**. This is not nationwide analytical coverage. The comparable monthly panel currently covers MA sportsbook and MI sportsbook/casino; NY remains a separate weekly panel. **The review ends in July 2026:** 12 monthly reads cover August 2025–July 2026; the latest three-month comparison is May–July versus the same months in 2025. Legal events are a small sourced watchlist, with historical status dates and recheck needs visible.

FLUT means native FanDuel evidence in the covered US states. DKNG means the native DraftKings brand/license, excluding Golden Nugget. CZR's supported panel covers MA/NY sportsbook and explicitly reviewed Michigan licensed digital operations. Grand Traverse sportsbook begins with full-month May 2021; the combined Grand Traverse/Sault casino series begins July 2024. Its land-based group remains outside the state panel. Handle, gross revenue, adjusted revenue, taxable revenue and issuer accounting are different measures. The dated Q2 issuer context can contradict an inference of profit improvement from positive wagering growth.

Use the root [launch and validation commands](../README.md). The default gaming checker runs 20, 90, 94 and the historical approval studies 91–93. **91–93 remain BLOCKED because their exact historical databases are missing.** Those gates and approval hashes have not changed. `--include-reference` also runs source examples and optional FLUT experiments 95/96; they are not daily prerequisites.
