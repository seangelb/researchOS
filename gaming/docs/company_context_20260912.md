## Tax and legal context

New Jersey's retained [July 2026 report](https://www.nj.gov/oag/ge/docs/Financials/PressRelease2026/July2026.pdf) continues to show the 19.75% base online gaming/sports wagering rates. They are existing context, not a newly announced September shock; separate tax provisions mean this is not an all-in effective rate. Its completed-event wagering statistics also show July handle up 23.6% year over year while year-to-date handle was down 3.6%. This is a useful counterexample to extrapolating a strong month, but it is a separate statewide measure and is not blended into MA/MI online handle.

The [IGB per-wager FAQ](https://igb.illinois.gov/content/dam/soi/en/web/igb/documents/press-releases/additional-news/20250617-faqs-on-new-statutory-sports-wager-tax.pdf), dated June 17, 2025, explains the July 1 start: $0.25 for the first 20 million online wagers and $0.50 thereafter per licensee in a July–June fiscal year. The charge is additional to other taxes, accrues when a wager is placed, and does not itself alter adjusted gross sports wagering receipts. Company cost cannot be estimated from handle dollars alone. This retained FAQ establishes the rule it describes, not an exhaustive audit of subsequent changes.

The existing [legal event register](../config/legal_events.json) separately retains the August 28, 2026 Ninth Circuit preliminary-injunction disposition. Subsequent stays, appeals and mandates have not been established in this review. Do not treat it as a final nationwide prohibition or assign a uniform positive/negative earnings effect to these companies.
